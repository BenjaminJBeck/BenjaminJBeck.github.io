About the Application

Oldly Notebook is a playful desktop journal that makes note-taking, journaling, and doodling fun and effortless. Import photos and videos and watch them take on a cozy, vintage-journal style. Sketch with the Draw tool, add unlimited pages and chapters, and navigate with tabs or a built-in Table of Contents. Simple, visual, and organized—your memories, your way. This is a first version, so it's not perfect yet! It is a work in progress, but I have lots of future update plans.

How to Use

Getting started with OldlyNotebook is incredibly simple. Follow these easy steps to begin your journey:

1️⃣

Download & Install

Download the application from the link below, it will automatically install on your desktop!

2️⃣

Open application

Open up the application!

3️⃣

Start Using

Start creating notebooks and journals!

To begin creating notebooks, click the stack of book making supplies in the top left. This will lead you to be able to change the name of the book and style. To open a book, simply click on it and follow the "Open Book" Button. To delete, drag the book over the trashcan.
